# Phase 1: Formal Statement of P vs NP

## Conjecture

P ⋚ NP?

Equivalently: does there exist a polynomial-time deterministic algorithm for SAT (or any other NP-complete problem)?

## Negation

P ≠ NP means no such algorithm exists.

## Formal Statement (Axiomatic)

Expressed within AXIOM as the non-existence of a polynomial-time reduction collapsing non-deterministic polynomial verification bounds to deterministic simulation bounds across all bounded-depth circuit families.

## Status

No theorem asserting P = NP or P ≠ NP is known.

The statement "P = NP?" remains an open decision problem.

## Clay Mathematics Institute (September 2026)

Unsolved. Only the Poincaré conjecture among the seven Millennium Prize problems has been settled.
